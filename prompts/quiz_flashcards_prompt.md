Make flashcards for a short multiple-choice quiz that tests definitions, lists and headline results exactly as the professor presented them. Use the source titled "STUDY GUIDE" as the list of what to cover and the slide decks as the authority on wording.

Cover everything, with as many cards as it takes:
- One card for every term in the study guide's key-terms tables, new and earlier. Front: the term. Back: the definition in the professor's words, then the same idea in plain words.
- One reverse card for the most important terms. Front: the definition. Back: the term.
- One card for every list on the slides. Front: "Name the … (N items)". Back: the items in order.
- One card for every named study or person. Front: the study. Back: the setup in a few words and the result with its number.
- One card for each exercise's key numbers and what the exercise was built to teach.
- A few "which is NOT" cards. Front: a list with one fake item. Back: the fake and why.

Keep each side short enough to read in five seconds. Do not invent anything that is not in the sources.
