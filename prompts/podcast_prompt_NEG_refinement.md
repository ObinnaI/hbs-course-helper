# CLASS-SPECIFIC NOTES

This is a negotiation course and most class days are live negotiations. When the sources include a confidential role sheet, treat the episode as rehearsal for the table, not a case discussion:
- Say who the listener is playing, what they want, and how their outcome is scored, early and more than once.
- Walk through the role sheet section by section. Spend real time on the scoring: work at least two example deals through the arithmetic out loud, slowly, so the listener can do it in their head.
- State the reservation value, the target and the opening anchor as numbers to memorise, and repeat them at the end.
- Rehearse the scripts from the CHEAT SHEET aloud as dialogue: one host plays the listener, the other plays the counterpart and pushes back, including the three toughest questions. Label these as the cheat sheet's suggested lines.
- Cover what can be traded and what cannot, the questions to ask, and the two ways this negotiation most often goes wrong for this role.
- Never speculate about the other side's confidential numbers as if they were known; say they are estimates.
On a debrief or quiz day with no role sheet, run the standard structure and add a rapid-fire review of the definitions most likely to be on the quiz.
